## Takeaways
1. A depth-varying sigma recovers most of the Gaussian deficit, but the Gaussian models still trail the lognormal ones on both splits.
2. The 1 m lognormal histogram has the lowest RMSE and the second-best seen-date elpd. It drops to -18181 on unseen dates, against -14573 for the lognormal smooth, so the per-bin effects do not transfer to new dates.
3. On every comparison here, 1 m bins add 10–25 h of fitting and give no gain over 5 m bins. The one exception is coverage of the Gaussian bin means. The 1 m DCM-depth model is equal to the 5 m one or worse, and 6 casts have their maximum at a boundary.
4. Letting the distance effect vary with depth adds nothing.
5. Combinations not yet tested: a lognormal half model with sigma by depth, and the 5 m lognormal histogram.
