## round3 design (2026-11-09), installer 06_create_round3.sh
Gage asked for (A) Gaussian versions of the models he's tried, and (B) new Bayesian models that treat depth as separate bins ("a histogram rather than a density plot"), with the same covariates and random effects.
Reading used for (A): shape150 was already all Gaussian, so the non-Gaussian ones are the lognormal next150 cases. Round3 refits each with a Gaussian likelihood on chl_norm and keeps every other setting. All cases use p20, primary + date-blocked seed 20261022, 4x(1000+2000), adapt_delta 0.95.
| Cases | Model | Data | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | bin_means: Gaussian mean of each 5 m bin | 5 m bin medians | (1+mld_z‖bin) + (1+mld_z‖Region:bin), distance, date+ENSO; sigma ~ Region + bin (fixed); scored on raw rows like the binned pair |
| 3-4 | bin_class: ordinal (cumulative logit) enhancement class per bin | 5 m bin medians | classes <+10%, +10-50, +50-100, +100-200, >=+200%; gives P(bin reaches each class) |
| 5-6 | dcm_depth: ordinal distribution of which 10 m bin holds each cast's DCM | 1 row per cast | mld_z + (0+mld_z‖Region), distance, date+ENSO |
| 7-16 | Gaussian versions of next150 3-4, 7-8, 9-10, 11-12, 13-14 | raw | sigma-depth, GI, pooled regional MLD, distance-vary, peak profile (chl_norm = background + peak - 1) |
- In the bin models, population predictions keep the bin terms and drop only date/ENSO (population_re). The bin_means sigma uses fixed bin effects, because re_formula would drop group-level sigma terms (seen in the local test).
- Ordinal scoring: held-out lpd per row and joint per date, RPS, Brier score at each "at least class k", modal accuracy, calibration by Region x bin. dcm_depth also gets DCM depth MAE (m) and 80% coverage.
- Plots: bin_probability_median_mld.png, bin_probability_mld_slices.png, dcm_depth_distribution.png.

## bins1m design (2026-11-09), installer 07_create_bins1m.sh
Gage wants the histogram approach at 1 m, not 5 m. (Round3 used 5 m bin medians for bin means and bin class, and 10 m classes for DCM depth.)
New experiment bins1m: every measured depth from 10 to 150 m is its own bin (factor depth_m, 141 levels). Raw 1 m rows are fitted directly, with no medians. p20, primary + date-blocked seed 20261022, same priors and settings as round3.
| Cases | Model | Data |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | bin_means Gaussian, (1+mld_z‖depth_m) + (1+mld_z‖Region:depth_m), sigma ~ Region + depth_m | ~30k raw rows |
| 3-4 | bin_class ordinal (same 5 enhancement classes) | ~30k raw rows |
| 5-6 | dcm_depth ordinal over 141 1 m classes (DCM = depth of the cast's max raw chl_norm) | 216 casts |
| 7-8 | bin_means lognormal (ratio = chl_norm+1), added because lognormal won next150 | ~30k raw rows |
Caveats: adjacent 1 m rows are highly correlated (~0.97), so 1 m bins will give overconfident intervals compared with 5 m. The 1 m DCM model spreads 216 casts over 141 classes, so its histogram will be spiky and many thresholds rest on the prior. Code is backward-compatible: the round3 cases build identical formulas.
